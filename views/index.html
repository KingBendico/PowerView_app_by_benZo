PowerView App — help

This app controls Hunter Douglas PowerView shades and scenes through your primary gateway on your home network.

Home

Home brings whole-home controls, favorite scenes, pinned shades and room shortcuts together. Star a shade in Blinds to pin it. Single-shade presets use percent closed; dual-rail presets use the bottom edge’s percentage from the top. Reported values come from the gateway; command acceptance does not mean motion is complete.

Blinds

Pick a room, then adjust each shade. Rooms can be sorted by gateway order, A–Z, or recent. Drag to choose the dashed target, then release to move. The solid fabric follows movement; between gateway reports its position is marked approximate. The final report settles the graphic. For dual-rail shades, drag either circular edge handle directly. When the edges meet, pull up for the top edge or down for the bottom edge. Reversing a drag keeps the same edge. Fine adjustment contains independent numeric positions and bottom-edge nudges/presets. Open shade, Close shade and Stop control the whole shade.

Appearance

Use the palette icon on a shade to choose blinds or curtains, fabric style and color. For curtains, choose a center-opening pair or a single curtain opening to the left or right. Curtains drag horizontally; blinds drag vertically. Choices are saved per device and home. Real two-rail devices keep both rails visible. Demo devices can switch to curtains and back; Reset restores their original controls and appearance.

Saved controls & temporary privacy

On Home → Saved controls, save current reported positions as a named preset or create a group of selected shades across rooms. These can also be assigned global shortcuts. Temporary privacy closes a selection, starts a countdown once closure is confirmed, and restores the previous positions. Keep PowerView running and your computer awake. New commands, reported changes, connection loss, sleep or quitting cancel the return. Restore now returns early; Cancel timer keeps the current positions. Existing Stop controls halt physical movement.

Scenes

Use Scenes in the navigation to run gateway scenes. Star favorites and use recent chips for quick access.

Schedules, health & activity

Schedules shows your saved PowerView routines, with weekday and enabled/paused filters. Clock times stay as saved in your home; sunrise/sunset entries keep their relative offsets. Check highlighted setup errors in the PowerView mobile app, where schedules are managed. Health shows device availability, approximate battery ranges, power source, reported signal and firmware. Its Activity view separates app command acceptance from gateway reports and failures. It keeps up to 200 entries per home during this app session; quitting clears the history.

Global keyboard shortcuts

Open Settings → Keyboard shortcuts to assign your own keys to any shade, a whole room, the whole home, or a scene. You can also show/hide PowerView or refresh its status. Shortcuts work while PowerView is running, even when another app is focused. Quitting releases them. They are saved separately for each gateway and the demo. Shortcuts pause while this editor is open; Save applies your changes. Percentage actions use percent closed.

Connection & settings

Set the primary gateway address under the gear icon (settings opens in this window). Use Test connection or Open Swagger UI if you need to check the hub — Swagger on port 3002 lists every REST path (scenes, shades, rooms, automation, and more) and is the best place to discover new capabilities. A yellow bar means the app cannot reach the gateway (wrong IP, hub offline, or network issue).

Tips

  • Theme: toggle light and dark with the sun/moon button on the main window.
  • Command+K or Ctrl+K focuses search. Find a room or shade, or run a scene. Use arrow keys to choose a result and Enter to open it.
  • Escape closes settings or help if they are open; otherwise it leaves a room's shade view and returns to all rooms.
  • Ctrl+B / Ctrl+S jump to Blinds or Scenes.
  • Ctrl+1 through Ctrl+9 open the 1st–9th room in the current Blinds grid order (Gateway / A–Z / Recent). Use the Control key (not Command on Mac). Top-row and numpad digits both work while this window is focused. Number badges on tiles match the shortcut.
  • Help opens on top of this window — press Escape, tap Close, or click the dimmed area outside the panel to dismiss.

Finding the gateway IP

On your router's device list, look for PowerView-G3 (or similar). Gateway discovery checks identity; the address order does not indicate the primary. Your hub may also answer at powerview-g3.local.
